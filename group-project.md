1  Executive Summary 

Company information 

Testudo is proposed as an Australian small IT business providing a risk-first cryptocurrency trading and performance platform. It will combine trade preparation, exchange connectivity, risk validation, journalling and analytics in one workflow. The service will not predict prices or guarantee returns; it will help users apply consistent rules before and after each trade. A demonstrable prototype will be developed by the 21st of August. 

Market opportunity 

Cryptocurrency traders often move between charting software, exchange dashboards, calculators, spreadsheets and journals. This can create inconsistent position sizing, manual errors and slow performance review. Testudo will turn a proposed trade into a structured process: the user will enter trade details, the platform will compare them with preset limits, and the outcome will be recorded for analysis. The initial market will be retail traders seeking discipline and convenience. 

Financial data 

The proposed model will use a free trial followed by subscriptions of approximately $19 or $39 per month. Illustrative Year 1 figures are $48,000 revenue, $41,000 operating expenses and $7,000 profit. These are planning assumptions that will require customer interviews, pricing research, supplier estimates and accounting advice before launch. 

2  Environment and Industry Analysis 

Target customers 

The primary customers will be intermediate and active retail cryptocurrency traders who use charting software or exchange APIs and want better control over leverage, position size and daily losses. Beginners may also use a paper-trading mode, although advanced automation will require clear explanations of its limits and risks. 

Competitors 

Testudo will compete with exchange dashboards, portfolio trackers, trading journals and automated platforms. Exchange dashboards support execution but usually focus on one venue. Trackers and journals help with review but may not control risk before a trade, while automated platforms may prioritise strategy results. Testudo will link preparation, risk checks and performance review in one system. 

Market forces 

Major market forces will include cryptocurrency volatility, rapid technology change, exchange API availability, cybersecurity, customer trust and regulation. Dependence on third-party exchanges may also cause interruptions or data changes. Testudo will therefore require clear limitations, flexible operating costs and secure handling of account and trading information. 

Product outlook and potential 

The August prototype will focus on core functions that can be developed and tested reliably within three weeks. Later versions could add more exchanges, mobile access, team accounts, AI-assisted coaching and advanced reports.  

3  Products or Services 

Does the solution satisfy the requirements of the target market? 

The solution will address practical trading problems rather than forecast cryptocurrency prices. Planned controls will include maximum account risk, stop-loss requirements, leverage limits, position sizing and daily drawdown limits. A browser extension and web dashboard will support trade preparation, warnings and journalling. This matches the target market’s need for faster and more consistent risk decisions. Effectiveness will be assessed through scenario-based testing and user feedback. 

Look, feel, features, benefits and uniqueness 

Testudo will use a clean, dark and data-focused interface suited to charting. Planned features include chart-based trade preparation, exchange connectivity, automated risk validation, journalling, performance charts and AI-assisted feedback. Its benefit will be convenience with discipline: users will remain in a familiar workflow while automation stays within their selected safeguards. This risk-first design will be the main point of difference. 

4  Compliance 

Registrations, licensing, taxation and legal requirements 

The proposed structure will be an Australian proprietary limited company, subject to legal and accounting advice, because the service may grow beyond one owner and carries commercial and cybersecurity risks. Before launch, the business will register its company and business name, obtain an ACN and ABN, maintain records and register for GST if required. It will also need terms of service, a privacy policy, cybersecurity procedures and clear risk disclaimers. Professional advice will confirm any financial-services, consumer-law, privacy, AUSTRAC and overseas obligations. These are proposed requirements, not completed approvals. 

5  Marketing, Research and Evaluation 

Target market 

Research will target Australian cryptocurrency traders who use charting software or exchange APIs. Interviews and a small prototype trial will examine risk-management problems, preferred features, pricing and security concerns. Project evaluation will measure task completion, usability, warning clarity and demonstration reliability. Commercial measures such as conversion and retention will apply only after launch. 

Branding 

The name Testudo refers to a protective formation, supporting a brand based on defence, discipline and controlled exposure. Marketing will avoid aggressive profit claims and instead use calm language, structured layouts and evidence-based reporting. 

Unique selling proposition 

Testudos's unique selling proposition is: “Testudo combines chart-based trade preparation, automated risk checks, exchange connectivity and performance journalling in one risk-first platform.” 

Look, feel and features 

Marketing material will show the planned browser extension, risk panel, journal and analytics dashboard. It will emphasise faster preparation, consistent sizing and easier review. Information regarding effective trading strategies and methods will also be included. 

Advertising and promotion 

Promotion could use educational posts, short demonstrations, cryptocurrency communities, referrals and small trading educators. A free trial and paper-trading mode may reduce perceived risk. Paid advertising will begin with a limited budget and will be assessed against customer acquisition cost and expected subscription value. 

Additional features 

The proposed website will include social links, a product demonstration, frequently asked questions, performance charts, security explanations and clear contact details. A location map is unnecessary because Testudo will operate online.  

6  Manufacturing and Operations Plan, Management Team, Timeline and Exit Strategy 

Management team 

The proposed team will consist of two members with complementary responsibilities. The Business Lead will manage market research, competitor analysis, pricing, financial planning, marketing, documentation and the overall project schedule. The Technical Lead will be responsible for platform design, software development, exchange integrations, automated risk-management features, AI-agent integration, testing, cybersecurity and system maintenance. Both members will contribute to user testing, decision-making and the final presentation. Legal and accounting services may be outsourced later to control costs. 

M&O framework 

For this digital service, manufacturing and operations will describe how the software is designed, built, tested and delivered. Work will use short development cycles, a prioritised feature list and regular reviews. The team will confirm minimum requirements, complete the highest-value workflow first and defer lower-priority functions.  

System integration, testing and data collection 

Operations will connect the browser interface, risk controls, journal and analytics components. Testing will cover successful trades, failed connections, incorrect order details, breached limits and recovery after interruption. Test credentials or paper trading will be used wherever possible. Only demonstration data will be collected, access will be restricted to the team, and results will support the final evaluation.
