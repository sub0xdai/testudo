# Referral Program: The Cohort

*"The formation grows stronger with every shield."*

---

## Why This Program Will Work

Crypto traders already share in groups. They have Discord servers, Telegram chats, X group DMs where they share setups, call trades, and compare results. Testudo's flex assets (Dignitas cards, P&L calendars) are already designed to be shared. The referral program adds a **selfish incentive** on top of the **social behavior that already exists**.

**Key insight:** Traders don't refer tools because they're generous. They refer tools because:
1. They want their friends on the same platform (so they can compare Dignitas scores)
2. They get something tangible for it
3. It signals they're serious about risk management (identity reinforcement)

---

## Program Structure: The Cohort System

### Core Mechanic

Every Testudo user gets a **Cohort Link** — a unique referral URL tied to their wallet address. When someone signs up through that link, both parties are connected in a "cohort" — a permanent on-chain relationship.

**Why "Cohort" not "referral":** Trading groups think in crews, not leads. "Join my cohort" is tribal language. "Use my referral link" is marketing language. Traders will say the first one. They won't say the second.

### The Referral Link

```
testudo.vip/join/[short-code]
```

Short code is derived from the referrer's wallet (first 6 chars + checksum). Example: `testudo.vip/join/0xC285a3`. Clean, memorable, looks on-chain native.

## Reward Tiers: What Traders Actually Value

### Psychology Applied

Traditional referral rewards (cash, credits, discounts) fail for this audience because:
- **$5 off** feels cheap to a trader moving thousands in leverage
- **Account credit** has no meaning in a $20/mo product
- **"Free month"** devalues the product ("it's only worth one month?")

What traders ACTUALLY value:
1. **Edge** — anything that makes them trade better
2. **Status** — visible proof they're better than others
3. **Access** — getting features/data others don't have
4. **Speed** — anything that removes friction from their workflow

### Tier Structure

| Tier | Name | Requirement | Rewards |
|------|------|-------------|---------|
| **0** | Legionary | Has account | Cohort link active. No rewards yet. |
| **1** | Decanus | 3 activated referrals | **Unlock: Extended Chart Pack** — 3 additional chart types in ChartSelector (holding period analysis, return distribution, time heatmap) |
| **2** | Optio | 7 activated referrals | **Unlock: Advanced Dignitas** — expanded radar with 8 axes (adds Consistency + Recovery Factor). Custom Dignitas card theme for sharing. |
| **3** | Centurion | 15 activated referrals | **Unlock: Multi-Timeframe Analytics** — weekly/monthly/quarterly performance breakdowns. Session replay (review your trading day trade-by-trade). |
| **4** | Tribunus | 30 activated referrals | **Lifetime access** — $20/mo waived permanently. Priority feature requests. Name in the credits. |

**"Activated referral"** = referred user connects wallet + adds exchange + executes first trade. Not just a signup — a real trader who reached activation. This prevents gaming.

### What the Referred Person Gets

Every person who signs up via a Cohort link gets:
- **7-day free trial** (no card required — wallet auth means no billing friction)
- **Instant Legionary role** in Discord (skips Recruit)
- **Cohort badge** on their desk profile showing who recruited them

This is the **double-sided reward** that psychology says converts better. The referrer gets unlocks. The referred gets a free trial + instant community status.

## Psychology Deep Dive: Why Each Reward Works

### Tier 1 — Extended Chart Pack (3 referrals)

**Models:** Endowment Effect, Loss Aversion, IKEA Effect

Once a trader has the extended charts and builds their analysis workflow around them, taking them away feels like a loss. The charts become "theirs." They've invested time customizing their overview layout. This creates switching cost — they won't leave Testudo because they'd lose their analytics setup.

**Why 3 referrals:** Low enough to feel achievable after sharing with their immediate trading group (most traders have 3-5 close trading friends). **Goal-Gradient Effect** — "You're 1 away from unlocking Extended Charts" creates pull.

### Tier 2 — Advanced Dignitas (7 referrals)

**Models:** Mimetic Desire, Social Proof, Status

The custom Dignitas card theme is the key. When a trader shares an Advanced Dignitas card on X, it looks *different* from the standard card. Other traders notice. "How do you have that version?" "You need 7 referrals." This triggers **mimetic desire** — they want the card because someone they respect has it.

The 8-axis radar is also genuinely more useful (adds Consistency and Recovery Factor). This is a real analytical upgrade, not just cosmetic. The reward has **intrinsic value**, not just status value.

**Why 7 referrals:** This is the "trading group" tier. A trader who shares Testudo with their Discord server or Telegram group can hit 7 easily if the product is good. It maps to the natural size of a close trading circle.

### Tier 3 — Multi-Timeframe Analytics (15 referrals)

**Models:** Authority Bias, Commitment & Consistency, Switching Costs

Session replay and multi-timeframe breakdowns are **professional-grade features**. Having them signals "I take this seriously." At 15 referrals, the trader has significant **sunk investment** in the platform — they've built their cohort, their data is deep, their analytics are sophisticated. Switching to another tool means losing all of this.

**Why 15 referrals:** This requires active evangelism beyond a single friend group. The trader needs to share publicly — X posts, Discord recommendations, trading community endorsements. This is where referral becomes **organic marketing**.

### Tier 4 — Lifetime Access (30 referrals)

**Models:** Reciprocity, Scarcity (earned), Peak-End Rule

Lifetime access is the ultimate reward. It's worth $240/year forever. But you can't buy it — you can only earn it. This makes it more valuable than the $50 Early Legionary pass. **Scarcity through effort** is more powerful than scarcity through time.

At 30 referrals, this person has brought in 30 activated traders. At $20/mo each, that's $600/mo in recurring revenue. Giving them lifetime access ($20/mo cost) is a 30:1 ROI. The math works beautifully.

## Trigger Moments: When to Prompt the Referral

### High-Intent Moments (In-App Prompts)

| Moment | Prompt | Psychology |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| First winning trade closes | "Nice trade. Know someone who'd want this? Share your Cohort link." | **Peak-End Rule** — they're on a high |
| Dignitas score increases | "Your Dignitas went up. Share your card — let your crew see." | **Status** — they want to flex |
| Green P&L calendar month | "Green month. Share your calendar. [Share] [Cohort Link]" | **Social Proof** — green months are flex material |
| After 10th trade | "You're a Centurion now. 3 referrals unlocks Extended Charts." | **Goal-Gradient** — show what's next |
| Tier unlock | "DECANUS UNLOCKED. Extended Chart Pack active. 4 more referrals to Advanced Dignitas." | **Commitment & Consistency** — they're already invested |

### Low-Intent Moments (Don't Prompt)

- After a losing trade (never)
- During onboarding (too early — they haven't experienced value)
- On the pricing page (wrong context)
- More than once per session (annoying)


## Anti-Gaming Measures

| Attack | Defense |
|--------|---------|
| Fake wallets | Activation requires exchange connection + first trade (real exchange API call) |
| Self-referral | Referrer and referred can't share the same exchange account (same API key detection) |
| Churned referrals | Reward only counts while referred user is active (traded in last 30 days). If they churn, the count decreases and tier can downgrade. |
| Bot farms | Rate limit: max 5 referral signups per cohort per day. Wallet must hold >0 balance on any supported chain. |
| Incentive abuse | Rewards are feature unlocks, not cash. No financial incentive to game. You'd need to actually want the features. |

**Key anti-gaming principle:** The rewards are **product features**, not **money**. Gaming a feature unlock requires actually using the product. This self-selects for real traders, not referral farmers.

## Launch Sequence

### Phase 1: Soft Launch (Week 1-2)
- Enable cohort links for existing users
- No tiers yet — just tracking
- Test link sharing, signup flow, attribution
- Announce in Discord: "Your cohort link is live on /desk/cohort"

### Phase 2: Tier Rewards (Week 3-4)
- Announce tier system with rewards
- Email/notification to existing users: "You already have [X] referrals. [Y] more to unlock Extended Charts."
- Zeigarnik Effect: showing progress toward a goal they didn't know they were chasing

### Phase 3: Flex Integration (Week 5+)
- Cohort link embedded in Dignitas cards and P&L calendar shares
- Custom card themes for Tier 2+ users
- Public leaderboard: "Top Cohort Builders" (opt-in)

## Second-Order Effects

**Switching costs compound:** More referrals → more tiers unlocked → more features in use → harder to leave. A Centurion (Tier 3) with multi-timeframe analytics, 15 cohort members, and months of journal data has enormous switching cost.

**Network effects emerge:** Cohort members can compare Dignitas scores. Trading groups that all use Testudo have shared analytics vocabulary (R-multiples, Dignitas, formation). This creates **language lock-in** — their trading conversations are structured around Testudo's concepts.

**Content compounds:** Every tier unlock generates a share-worthy moment ("Just unlocked Advanced Dignitas!"). The referral program creates its own content calendar.
